### ⚾ Baseball  

#### Rules & How the Game Works  *(Eastern Shore nuances)*  
* **Pitch‑count limits** – MPSSAA caps high‑school pitchers at **85 pitches** per game; Star Democrat often notes “coach‑Mike Harper pulled his ace after 78 pitches to preserve eligibility for the district tournament.”  
* **Mercy rule** – 10‑run rule after 5 innings is enforced in regular‑season play; several playoff games have been **ended early** (e.g., 2023 Bayside North final, Easton 8 – 0 CSD).  
* **Conference tiebreakers** – Head‑to‑head record, then **run differential** in conference games; this has decided the 2022 Bayside South seed (Kent Island won on a +12 differential).  

#### Key Stats & What They Mean  
| Stat | Local relevance |
|------|-----------------|
| **ERA** | A sub‑1.00 ERA (e.g., 0.85 by Cambridge‑South Dorchester in 2021) often predicts a deep playoff run. |
| **OPS** | On‑base + slugging; a team OPS > .850 correlates with a top‑3 conference finish. |
| **K/9** | Strikeouts per 9 innings; high K/9 teams (e.g., Easton 9.2 in 2022) tend to dominate the **late‑inning mercy‑rule** games. |

#### What Decides Games on the Eastern Shore  
* **Field conditions** – Clay vs. artificial turf at the Kent Island Complex influences ground‑ball vs. fly‑ball strategies. <br>* **Travel fatigue** – Teams playing a Thursday away game in Dorchester then a Friday home game often see a **drop in batting average** (average 0.210 vs. 0.275 at home). <br>* **Weather** – Early‑spring rain can force **double‑headers**, testing bullpen depth (2020 “rain‑out‑to‑double‑header” saga). <br>* **Coaching style** – “Small‑ball” vs. “Power‑hitting” splits; Easton’s “manufacturing runs” (bunts, steals) vs. Kent Island’s “home‑run heavy” approach.  

#### Positions & Terminology  
* **Ace** – Starting pitcher expected to pitch the first 5–6 innings. <br>* **Closer** – Reliever used for the final 2–3 innings, often in “save” situations (MPSSAA rules require a 1‑run lead). <br>* **DH (Designated Hitter)** – Used in all Bayside games; the DH is a frequent source of “bench‑warmer to star” story arcs.  

#### What to Watch For  
* **Pitch‑count management** – Coaches often pull pitchers early to protect them for the **district tournament**; watch for “strategic pull” quotes. <br>* **Family dynasties** – The “Hollis” brothers (senior & freshman) have both started on the mound for Easton; a classic “brotherly rivalry” angle. <br>* **Field upgrades** – Recent 2023 bond‑issue funded a new dugout at Queen Anne’s County; stories on facility impact on recruitment.  

#### Interview Tips  
* **Ask about “next‑man‑up” philosophy** – many coaches (e.g., Coach Rogers, St. Michael’s) pride themselves on depth. <br>* **Frame questions around the “playoff clock”** – “How does the 85‑pitch limit shape your rotation heading into district?” <br>* **Highlight community support** – boosters often fund equipment; ask “What does the community mean to your program’s success?”  

#### Story Angles (Star Democrat‑style)  
* **“From the sandlot to the state finals”** – follow a freshman’s first year (e.g., 2024 Easton freshman pitcher). <br>* **“Rivalry re‑ignited: Easton vs. St. Michael’s – a 2025 preview”** – include past head‑to‑head stats, player quotes, and attendance projections. <br>* **“Coach Harper’s rebuild: after a 7‑year playoff drought, the Bayside North is back”** – focus on recruiting, training changes, and community fund‑raisers.

### 🏀 Basketball  

#### Rules & How the Game Works *(Eastern Shore nuances)*  
* **Bayside overtime** – If a conference game ends tied, a **single 2‑minute overtime** is played; the winner gets the full conference point (no split).  
* **Shot‑clock** – 30‑second MPSSAA clock (shorter than NCAA). Coaches often use “press‑break” sets at the 8‑second mark – a tactical point for story focus.  
* **Eligibility** – Transfer rules require a **90‑day sit‑out**; several recent disputes (2023 Kent Island transfer) generated front‑page coverage.  

#### Key Stats & What They Mean  
| Stat | Local relevance |
|------|-----------------|
| **PPS (Points per Shot)** | High PPS (≥ 1.10) for a team signals efficient offense; Kent Island’s 2022 PPS of 1.14 correlated with a conference title. |
| **Assist‑to‑Turnover Ratio** | > 2.0 indicates disciplined ball movement; Easton’s 2021 ratio of 2.3 was a hallmark of their “team‑first” identity. |
| **Rebound Margin** | Positive margin (≥ +5) often predicts victory in the physical Bayside South games. |

#### What Decides Games on the Eastern Shore  
* **Home‑court advantage** – The **“Crowd Noise Factor”** (average 800 fans) gives a measurable edge; Easton’s 2020 home record 15‑2 vs. 8‑7 on the road. <br>* **Travel schedule** – Some schools (e.g., Stephen Decatur) play two away games on a back‑to‑back Thursday–Friday, affecting fatigue. <br>* **Coaching style** – “Full‑court press” vs. “half‑court motion”; the press is a hallmark of Kent Island’s 2022 championship run. <br>* **Senior leadership** – Teams with a senior point guard (e.g., 2023 Queen Anne’s “Miller”) often have higher clutch‑time shooting percentages.  

#### Positions & Terminology  
* **Combo Guard** – Plays both point and shooting guard duties; increasingly common in Bayside “positionless” schemes. <br>* **Post‑up** – Traditional big man play; still vital for Queens Anne’s County’s inside game. <br>* **Pick‑and‑Roll** – Core to Kent Island’s offense; watch for “screen‑set” quotes from coaches.  

#### What to Watch For  
* **Rivalry intensity** – The **Easton vs Cambridge‑South Dorchester** game often sees “technical fouls” and “coach ejections”; perfect for drama. <br>* **Mid‑season tournament performance** – The **Bayside Invitational** (early Jan) can set the tone for conference seeding. <br>* **Freshman impact** – 2024 “10‑point debut” by a Kent Island freshman sparked a “new‑generation” narrative.  

#### Interview Tips  
* **Leverage the “press” narrative** – ask coaches “How does the full‑court press fit into your philosophy against a rival like Easton?” <br>* **Focus on “senior night”** – capture emotional quotes about legacy and community. <br>* **Explore “off‑court bonding”** – many teams do community service; this adds a human‑interest layer.  

#### Story Angles  
* **“Pressing for pride: Kent Island’s full‑court strategy that led to a 2022 state run.”** <br>* **“From bench to breakout: The freshman who changed Easton’s 2024 season.”** <br>* **“Coach Miller’s 15‑year run: How a small‑town program stays relevant.”**

### 🏑 Field Hockey  

#### Rules & How the Game Works *(Eastern Shore nuances)*  
* **Penalty‑corner limit** – MPSSAA caps **four** penalty‑corners per half; teams that earn the 5th must defend a **penalty‑stroke**. This rule has produced dramatic swings (e.g., 2023 Queen Anne’s five‑corner comeback vs. St. Peter & Paul). <br>* **Match length** – Two 35‑minute halves; overtime is **golden goal** (first goal wins). <br>* **Substitution window** – Unlimited “rolling” subs, but coaches often rotate entire lines every 5 minutes to keep legs fresh on the often‑wet grass fields.  

#### Key Stats & What They Mean  
| Stat | Local relevance |
|------|-----------------|
| **CPC (Corners per Game)** | High CPC (> 5) indicates aggressive attacking; Kent Island averaged 6.2 CPC in 2022, leading to a conference title. |
| **Save %** | Goalies with > 70 % save rate (e.g., Easton’s senior keeper in 2021) are usually All‑State selections. |
| **Face‑off win %** | > 55 % often correlates with possession dominance; Queen Anne’s County topped the league at 58 % in 2023. |

#### What Decides Games on the Eastern Shore  
* **Field condition** – Early‑season games on **wet grass** (e.g., the Easton vs. Stephen Decatur match in 2022) slow ball speed, favoring defensive teams. <br>* **Weather** – Fog on the Bay can cause “delayed start” and impact visibility for penalty‑corners. <br>* **Coaching emphasis** – Some programs (e.g., St. Peter & Paul) run a “high‑press” system; others (Cambridge‑South Dorchester) rely on **set‑piece efficiency**. <br>* **Depth of roster** – Because of the rolling‑sub rule, teams with **12+ active players** (e.g., Kent Island) sustain intensity longer.  

#### Positions & Terminology  
* **Drag‑flick** – The primary penalty‑corner shot; many Easton players specialize in this. <br>* **Sweeper** – Defensive anchor; Queen Anne’s County’s veteran sweeper is a frequent All‑State pick. <br>* **Midfield engine** – Player covering both attack and defense; crucial in the fast‑transition style of Kent Island.  

#### What to Watch For  
* **Penalty‑corner specialists** – Their success often decides close games (e.g., 2024 St. Peter & Paul’s “Miller” with 3 goals in a 2‑minute corner). <br>* **Rivalry “first‑goal” tradition** – In the Easton‑vs‑Cambridge‑South Dorchester rivalry, the **first goal** is traditionally scored by a senior; a deviation makes for a compelling human‑interest note. <br>* **Club‑team pipeline** – Many starters come from the **Eastern Shore Field‑Hockey Club**; a story on that feeder system is evergreen.  

#### Interview Tips  
* **Ask about “corner preparation”** – coaches love describing the drill routine (e.g., “We practice the drag‑flick 30 times before each game”). <br>* **Probe community support** – many field‑hockey programs rely on **local “girls‑sports” grants**; ask “How do community funds shape your off‑season training?” <br>* **Highlight multi‑sport athletes** – many field‑hockey players also play **soccer**; a dual‑sport angle can illustrate the region’s athletic culture.  

#### Story Angles  
* **“Drag‑flick dominance: How Kent Island’s corner specialist turned a 3‑0 deficit into a 4‑3 win.”** <br>* **“From the club to the crown: The pipeline that fuels Queen Anne’s County’s field‑hockey success.”** <br>* **“Rain‑softened turf, hardened resolve: Easton’s championship run on a soggy field.”**

### 🏈 Football  

#### Rules & How the Game Works *(Eastern Shore nuances)*  
* **Mercy rule** – MPSSAA enforces a **35‑point lead** after the 3rd quarter in 1A/2A games; several early‑season shutouts (e.g., 2021 Kent Island 42‑0) have triggered it. <br>* **Play‑clock adjustments** – In the **Bayside Conference**, officials add **30 seconds** after each timeout to accommodate the long travel distances between fields. <br>* **Transfer eligibility** – A 90‑day sit‑out rule; the 2023 Kent Island‑Stephen Decatur dispute led to a district hearing that the Star Democrat covered extensively.  

#### Key Stats & What They Mean  
| Stat | Local relevance |
|------|-----------------|
| **YDS/Play** | > 5.5 yards per play typically correlates with a **playoff berth** (Easton 2022). |
| **Turnover margin** | Positive margin (+2 or better) is a strong predictor of conference championships; Kent Island posted +5 in 2020. |
| **Third‑down conversion %** | > 45 % signals sustained drives; Queen Anne’s County’s 48 % in 2023 helped them win the **Bayside South** title. |

#### What Decides Games on the Eastern Shore  
* **Field surface** – Grass at Easton vs. **synthetic turf** at Kent Island; teams that practice on turf often have a **running‑play advantage** on synthetic fields. <br>* **Travel fatigue** – Teams that play a Thursday away game on the Eastern Shore’s **bridge** (e.g., crossing the Chesapeake Bay Bridge‑Tunnel) often see a **drop in offensive efficiency** the following Friday. <br>* **Weather** – Early‑season fog, mid‑season thunderstorms, and late‑season **snow‑melt** can cause **game delays** and affect footing (2022 “mud‑slide” game vs. Stephen Decatur). <br>* **Coaching philosophy** – “Run‑heavy, ball‑control” (Easton) vs. “Air‑raid, spread” (Kent Island). The contrast fuels rivalry storylines each year.  

#### Positions & Terminology  
* **Dual‑threat QB** – Common in the Shore; the 2023 Easton senior quarterback excelled both passing (2,200 yards) and rushing (900 yards). <br>* **Nickel/Dime packages** – Used heavily by Kent Island in 2A play; a good angle is the “defensive adaptation to spread offenses.” <br>* **Special teams “kick‑off return”** – Many star returners (e.g., Cambridge‑South Dorchester’s “Jackson”) become community heroes.  

#### What to Watch For  
* **Thanksgiving “classic” games** – Attendance spikes (often > 1,200); the **pre‑game pep‑talk** is a perfect quote source. <br>* **Coaching milestones** – Coach Rob Burnham’s 150th win (2022) created a “legacy” story. <br>* **Playoff droughts** – Princess Anne’s 10‑year absence from the state playoffs (last in 2013) provides a “redemption” narrative each season.  

#### Interview Tips  
* **Focus on “community impact”** – ask “What does Friday night mean for the town of Easton beyond the scoreboard?” <br>* **Highlight “player development”** – many seniors receive Division I offers; ask about the **academic‑athletic balance**. <br>* **Rivalry framing** – “How does the history of the Kent Island‑Colonel Richardson game shape this year’s preparation?”  

#### Story Angles  
* **“The O’Connor brothers: A family feud on the field and at home.”** (Easton vs. Kent Island quarterback showdown). <br>* **“From a 7‑year drought to the state semifinals: Princess Anne’s comeback story.”** <br>* **“Coach Burnham’s play‑book: The evolution of a 2A powerhouse.”**

### 🥍 Lacrosse  

#### Rules & How the Game Works *(Eastern Shore nuances)*  
* **Shot‑clock** – 60‑second possession clock for **men’s** lacrosse; **30‑second** for **women’s** (per MPSSAA). <br>* **Face‑off “draw”** – In the Bayside Conference, a **“quick‑draw”** rule allows the offensive team to restart within 5 seconds of a goal, accelerating the tempo. <br>* **Penalty length** – Minor penalties are **1 minute**; a “double‑minor” (2 minutes) is common for aggressive checking in the **“rough‑play”** culture of the Shore.  

#### Key Stats & What They Mean  
| Stat | Local relevance |
|------|-----------------|
| **Goals‑per‑Game (GPG)** | Teams averaging > 10 GPG (e.g., Kent Island 2022) usually finish in the top 2 of the Bayside. |
| **Caused Turnovers** | High CT (≥ 30 per season) signals aggressive defense; Easton’s 2021 defense led the conference with 38 CT. |
| **Face‑off win %** | > 55 % often translates to **possession advantage**; Queen Anne’s County posted 60 % in 2023, fueling their championship run. |

#### What Decides Games on the Eastern Shore  
* **Fast‑pace vs. possession** – Kent Island favors a **fast‑break** style, while Queen Anne’s County relies on **slow, controlled ball movement**. <br>* **Weather** – Wind on the Bay can affect **ground‑ball** handling; a breezy day (e.g., 2022 “Wind‑swept final”) often leads to low‑scoring games. <br>* **Rivalry intensity** – The **Easton vs Kent Island** lacrosse clash has a 7‑year streak of one‑team dominance; each game is a “title‑implications” story. <br>* **Travel** – Some teams must cross the **Chesapeake Bay Bridge‑Tunnel** for a single conference game; travel fatigue influences late‑season performance.  

#### Positions & Terminology  
* **Attackman** – Primary scorer; Kent Island’s senior attackman leads the conference with 55 goals (2023). <br>* **Midfield “transition”** – Players who run the **fast‑break**; a hallmark of the “run‑and‑gun” style. <br>* **Goalie “puck‑out”** – Quick distribution after a save; essential for fast‑break teams.  

#### What to Watch For  
* **“Goalie duels”** – Rivalries often hinge on the **star goalies** (e.g., Easton vs. Queen Anne’s County 2021). <br>* **“Coach’s signature formation”** – Coach Miller’s “3‑3‑1 zone” at Kent Island is a frequent tactical talking point. <br>* **Multi‑sport athletes** – Many lacrosse players also star on the **football** team; a “dual‑sport star” profile resonates with readers.  

#### Interview Tips  
* **Probe “transition speed”** – “How do you train your midfielders to attack in under 5 seconds after a turnover?” <br>* **Explore “community roots”** – many players come from local club lacrosse; ask about the **club‑team-to‑high‑school pipeline**. <br>* **Ask about “weather preparation”** – “Do you adjust practice for wind on the Bay?”  

#### Story Angles

### ⚽ Soccer  

#### Rules & How the Game Works *(Eastern Shore nuances)*  
* **Golden‑goal overtime** – Used only in **post‑season** (district/state). Regular‑season ties stand as draws. <br>* **Substitution rule** – Unlimited “rolling” subs; most coaches rotate entire squads every **10 minutes** due to the **heat** on summer fields. <br>* **Pitch size** – Many Eastern Shore fields are **slightly narrower** (70 yd vs. standard 75 yd) because of historic community field constraints; this encourages **compact, possession‑based play**.  

#### Key Stats & What They Mean  
| Stat | Local relevance |
|------|-----------------|
| **Goals‑against average (GAA)** | Teams with GAA < 1.0 (e.g., Queen Anne’s County 2022) dominate the conference. |
| **Possession %** | > 55 % usually correlates with **clean-sheet** performances; Easton’s 2021 possession‑based style led to a 9‑0 win. |
| **Shots on target (SOT)** | High SOT (> 8 per game) indicates offensive pressure; Kent Island’s 2023 SOT of 10.2 helped them clinch the regular‑season title. |

#### What Decides Games on the Eastern Shore  
* **Field conditions** – Early‑spring games on **wet grass** often become **low‑scoring** affairs. <br>* **Heat & humidity** – Summer matches (June) see **player fatigue**; teams that bench early (after 60 min) usually have a **higher second‑half goal rate**. <br>* **Rivalry “turf wars”** – The **Easton vs. Cambridge‑South Dorchester** rivalry often sees a **“home‑field advantage”** of +1.5 goals due to familiarity with the narrow pitch. <br>* **Coaching philosophy** – “Possession + high press” (Easton) vs. “Direct counter‑attack” (Kent Island).  

#### Positions & Terminology  
* **Target forward** – Holds the ball up; a key role for Queen Anne’s County’s senior striker. <br>* **Box‑to‑box midfielder** – Covers both defense and attack; typical in the “all‑round” Easton system. <br>* **Sweeper‑keeper** – Goalkeepers who act as an extra defender; a trend in the Kent Island squad.  

#### What to Watch For  
* **Set‑piece mastery** – Many championship games are decided by a **corner kick**; Easton’s “corner‑corner” routine is a recurring storyline. <br>* **Youth academy output** – The **Eastern Shore Soccer Academy** feeds the top 3 programs; a “pipeline” feature is evergreen. <br>* **Cross‑sport conditioning** – Many soccer players also run track; a story on **speed training** can tie into the region’s track‑and‑field beat.  

#### Interview Tips  
* **Ask about “turf adaptation”** – “How does the narrower home field shape your tactical approach?” <br>* **Highlight “community support”** – Boosters often fund **field lighting upgrades**; ask “What does the new lighting mean for night games?” <br>* **Focus on “player development”** – many seniors earn **college scholarships**; ask “What does the recruitment process look like for a Shore athlete?”  

#### Story Angles

### 🤼‍♀️ Wrestling  

#### Rules & How the Game Works *(Eastern Shore nuances)*  
* **Weight‑class limits** – MPSSAA adheres to the **National Federation of State High School Associations** (NFHS) weight classes; the Eastern Shore often sees **high participation** in the **118 lb** and **138 lb** classes (most schools have 2–3 wrestlers in each). <br>* **Tournament seeding** – Based on **regular‑season win‑loss** and **head‑to‑head**; a **tiebreaker** of **most pins** is used in the Bayside Conference (2022 Easton earned the top seed with 12 pins). <br>* **Dual‑meet overtime** – Single **3‑minute** overtime; if still tied, the match is declared a **draw** (affects team points).  

#### Key Stats & What They Mean  
| Stat | Local relevance |
|------|-----------------|
| **Pin percentage** | High pin % (> 30 %) often leads to **team championships**; Kent Island’s 2021 team pinned 38 % of its matches. |
