## 1.  The Local Sports Landscape  
The Eastern Shore of Maryland is a tight‑knit patchwork of towns—Easton, Stevensville, Kent Island, Queen Anne’s County, Salisbury, and the surrounding rural communities.  Sports are a cultural glue, and lacrosse sits at the very heart of that fabric.

### Why Lacrosse Matters Most  
| Factor | What It Looks Like on the Eastern Shore |
|--------|----------------------------------------|
| **Community Tradition** | Lacrosse has been a varsity sport at almost every public high school for three decades. Families often grow up watching the same rivalries (e.g., Easton vs Kent Island, Saints Peter & Paul vs Easton) from the sidelines of the same field. |
| **Rivalries & Pride** | The **Bayside Conference**, **ESIAC**, and the **Talbot Lacrosse Association** generate week‑to‑week story hooks: “Warriors lock down on Sabres,” “Bucs strike quick for third title.”  The intensity of these rivalries fuels attendance, booster money, and social‑media buzz. |
