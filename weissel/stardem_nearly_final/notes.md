### **1️⃣ Baseball**

#### Rules & How the Game Works
- **Pitch‑Count Limits** – MPSSAA caps high‑school pitchers at **85 pitches per game**; the *Star‑Democrat* often notes when a star arm (e.g., Easton’s **J. Miller**) hits the limit and sits for the remainder of a series.  
- **Mercy Rule** – If a team leads by **10 runs after 5 innings**, the game is called – a frequent occurrence in early‑season non‑conference play.  
- **Conference Tiebreakers** – Head‑to‑head record, then **run differential in conference games** (used in the 2023 Bayside North tie between St. Michael’s and Easton).  

#### Key Stats & What They Mean
| Stat | Shore‑Specific Interpretation |
|------|-------------------------------|
| **ERA** | Low ERA (< 2.00) often indicates a **dominant local ace** (e.g., Kent Island’s 1.85 ERA in 2022) that can carry a team through district play. |
| **Slugging %** | High SLG (> .600) correlates with **power‑heavy lineups** common in South Bayside teams (Queen Anne’s, Kent Island). |
| **Fielding %** | Errors are magnified on **grass fields** that can become uneven after rain; a drop in fielding % often ties to **poor drainage at older venues** (e.g., Cambridge‑South Dorchester’s field). |

#### What Decides Games on the Eastern Shore
- **Weather & Field Conditions** – Summer rain can turn infield dirt to mud, favoring teams with **speed and small‑ball** tactics.  
- **Travel Fatigue** – Friday night away games (e.g., Easton at St. Michael’s) can affect performance on Saturday’s double‑header.  
- **Pitching Depth** – Smaller rosters mean a **single ace** can dominate the regular season; depth becomes crucial in district playoffs.  

#### Positions & Terminology
- **“The Ace”** – Starting pitcher expected to start the conference opener.  
- **“Mid‑week Rotation”** – Non‑conference games on Tuesdays/Thursdays used to **preserve the ace** for weekend conference play.  
- **“Bunt‑and‑Run”** – Common strategy on **slow infields** (e.g., Talbot County).  

#### What to Watch For
- **Pitch‑count management** stories (coach’s decision to pull a pitcher early).  
- **Rivalry “walk‑offs”** – 3‑run walk‑off in the final inning of Easton vs St. Michael’s is a headline staple.  
- **Summer league performance** – Players who excel in **town‑team leagues** often return with improved stats.  

#### Interview Tips
- **Ask about “family tradition”** – many players cite fathers/grandfathers who played on the same field.  
- **Probe the “coach’s philosophy”** – e.g., “Do you favor power hitting or small‑ball?” especially after a big win.  
- **Tie into community** – “How does the booster club’s support affect the program this year?”
