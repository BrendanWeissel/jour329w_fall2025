**STAR DEMOCRAT – UNIFIED HIGH SCHOOL SPORTS BEAT BOOK**  
*(Eastern Shore & Mid‑Shore, Maryland)*

## Overall Tips for Covering High School Sports on the Eastern Shore  

1. **Map the Geography & Rivalries** – The Bayside Conference (North & South), the Eastern Shore Independent Athletic Conference (ESIAC), and county‑line match‑ups (e.g., **Easton vs Kent Island**, **Cambridge vs Colonel Richardson**) drive attendance and story angles. Keep a quick‑reference map of travel times; a bridge‑crossing adds ~15 min.  

2. **Embed in the Community** – Booster‑club meetings, summer “town‑team” games, and PTA minutes reveal the same family names year after year. Attend at least one off‑season event per sport to build trust with coaches, ADs, and parents.  

3. **Master the Calendar** – Create a color‑coded master calendar (Google Sheet or paper) for all sports. Note overlap periods (spring: baseball, softball, lacrosse; fall: football, field hockey, soccer) and playoff windows. Update daily; a missed date costs a deadline.  

4. **Prioritize with the Impact‑Decision Matrix**  
   - **Rivalry intensity** (historical rivalry, homecoming)  
   - **Playoff/seed implications**  
   - **Milestone story** (coach’s 100th win, senior record)  
   - **Editor request**  
   Use the matrix to decide which live game to cover and which to recap via phone/stat sheet.  

5. **Build a Living Stats & Contact Database** – One shared spreadsheet per sport: roster, eligibility notes, injury updates, coach/AD cell numbers, and key season stats. Update after every game; it becomes your “beat bible.”  

6. **Recap Formula (300‑500 words)**  
   - **Lede** = decisive moment (walk‑off, overtime goal, shutout).  
   - **Quote** from player or coach within the first three paragraphs.  
   - **Key stats** (team line, top performer, season‑to‑date).  
   - **Context** (rivalry history, playoff picture).  

7. **Look Beyond the Box Score** – Senior profiles, coaching milestones, Title IX growth (girls’ wrestling, lacrosse), weather‑related field issues, and recruiting/college commitments are evergreen angles.  

8. **Eligibility & Safety** – Verify every name with the AD before publishing. For concussion or injury stories, obtain parent/AD permission and follow MPSSAA protocol.  

9. **Quick‑Action Photography** – If a photographer isn’t assigned, capture 3–5 high‑quality action shots on your phone for web galleries and social‑media teasers.  

10. **Fast Turn‑around** – Friday night games must be filed by Saturday morning. Draft the lede in‑game, lock in final stats with the official scorer, and file the story within two hours of the final whistle.  

11. **Gender Balance** – Track story counts by sport and gender quarterly; aim for parity, especially for softball, field hockey, and girls’ lacrosse.  

12. **Stay Visible** – Introduce yourself to every head coach at season‑opening meetings, share your cell, and return calls/texts within two hours—even on weekends.

## Baseball  

- **Key Stats to Track** – Runs, hits, errors (RHE line); batting average, RBI, ERA, pitch counts (MPSSAA limit 110 pitches/day).  
- **Decisive Factors** – Pitching depth (starters rarely exceed 5 innings), timely hitting with runners in scoring position, defensive miscues late in games, weather‑related field conditions.  
- **Local Highlights** – Rivalries: **Cambridge vs St. Mary’s (St. Mary’s County)**, **Easton vs Kent Island**; standout programs: *Choptank, Kent County, Queen Anne’s*.  
- **Story Angles** – Pitching duels, walk‑off victories, senior pitchers heading to college, double‑header due to rain, “late‑bloomer” breakout hitters.  
- **Interview Prompts** – “What was working for you on the mound today?”; “How did the defense affect your approach at the plate?”; coach on pitch‑count strategy.

## Basketball  

- **Key Stats** – Points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks; shooting percentages (FG %, 3P %, FT %); turnovers.  
- **Decisive Factors** – Free‑throw shooting in close games, defensive intensity in the final quarter, halftime adjustments, foul trouble of star players.  
- **Local Highlights** – Powerhouses: *Cambridge‑Sudler, Kent Island, St. Mary’s (St. Mary’s County), Queen Anne’s*. Notable rivalry: **Cambridge vs Colonel Richardson** (annual “Battle of the Bay”).  
- **Story Angles** – Milestone coach wins, buzzer‑beaters, sibling legacies on rosters, transfer‑student impacts, playoff‑seed battles.  
- **Interview Prompts** – “What was the turning point?”; “How did the crowd influence the free‑throw line?”; coach on defensive sets used at halftime.

## Field Hockey  

- **Key Stats** – Goals, assists, saves, penalty‑corner (PC) attempts & conversions, shots on goal.  
- **Decisive Factors** – PC execution (≈ 50 % conversion rate), midfield possession, goalkeeper performance, “clog‑the‑circle” defense.  
- **Local Highlights** – Dominant programs: *St. Mary’s (St. Mary’s County), Kent Island, Cambridge‑Southern*. Rivalry: **St. Mary’s vs Easton** (state‑tournament qualifiers).  
- **Story Angles** – Senior goalkeeper heroics, PC‑specialist profiles, unbeaten streaks, club‑team pipelines, rise of girls’ lacrosse affecting field‑hockey participation.  
- **Interview Prompts** – “Walk me through today’s penalty‑corner set‑up.”; “What adjustments did you make after halftime?”; goalie on positioning for the final period.

## Football  

- **Key Stats** – TDs, FG, PAT, total offense yards, third‑down conversions, turnover margin, time‑of‑possession.  
- **Decisive Factors** – Turnovers, red‑zone efficiency, special‑teams play (kick returns, blocked kicks), fourth‑quarter clock management.  
- **Local Highlights** – Traditional powerhouses: *Cambridge, Kent Island, Choptank, St. Mary’s (St. Mary’s County)*. Rivalry: **Cambridge vs Colonel Richardson** (homecoming).  
- **Story Angles** – Playoff‑seed scenarios, coach career‑win milestones, quarterback duels (often family ties), injury comebacks, “Battle of the Bridges” (games requiring crossing the Chesapeake).  
- **Interview Prompts** – “What was the key play that swung momentum?”; “How did the weather affect your game plan?”; coach on line‑of‑scrimmage battles.

## Lacrosse  

- **Key Stats** – Goals, assists, saves, ground balls, face‑off win % (boys), man‑up/man‑down goals.  
- **Decisive Factors** – Face‑off dominance, goalie save percentage (> 60 % strong), clearing efficiency, capitalizing on man‑up chances.  
- **Local Highlights** – Strong programs: *Cambridge, Kent Island, St. Mary’s (St. Mary’s County), Queen Anne’s*. Rivalry: **Cambridge vs Kent Island** (often determines conference champion).  
- **Story Angles** – Breakout attackmen, goalie duels, sibling rivalries, expansion of girls’ lacrosse (Title IX), state‑tournament runs.  
- **Interview Prompts** – “How did the face‑off unit affect possession today?”; “What was your approach on the man‑up power play?”; coach on defensive ride adjustments.

## Soccer  

- **Key Stats** – Goals, assists, shots on goal, saves, corner kicks, fouls, clean sheets.  
- **Decisive Factors** – Midfield possession, set‑piece execution, goalkeeper heroics, fitness in the final 15 minutes.  
- **Local Highlights** – Competitive schools: *Cambridge, Kent Island, St. Mary’s (St. Mary’s County), Choptank*. Rivalry: **Cambridge vs St. Mary’s** (often decides district champion).
