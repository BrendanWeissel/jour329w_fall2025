## 1. Local Sports Landscape Overview  

### The Eastern Shore’s Athletic DNA  
The Eastern Shore of Maryland (the counties of Queen Anne’s, Kent, Caroline, Dorchester, Talbot and Wicomico) is a patchwork of small‑town pride, tight‑knit school districts and a surprisingly deep love for a handful of high‑school sports.  

| Sport | Why it matters | Typical hot‑spots |
|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Field Hockey** | Long‑standing girls’ programs, strong feeder systems through club and travel teams, and a tradition of “penalty‑corner” excellence. The sport is a year‑round conversation in PTA meetings, booster clubs and even the local hardware store. | Queen Anne’s County, Kent Island, Easton, St. Peter & Paul, Holly Grove, Pocomoke, Stephen Decatur |
| Basketball (boys & girls) | Large venues, regional tournaments, and a pipeline to D‑I scholarships. | Easton, Cambridge, Salisbury |
| Baseball / Softball | Summer leagues, community “town‑team” pride, and a sizable alumni following. | Salisbury, Cambridge, Princess Anne |
| Soccer (boys & girls) | Growing participation, especially with the influx of club teams from the Baltimore metro area. | Kent Island, Queen Anne’s, Salisbury |

Field hockey dominates the fall schedule (late August – early November) and again during the spring “club” season (April – June). The sport’s rhythm is dictated by **conference play** (North Bayside, Eastern Shore Independent Athletic Conference, etc.), **regional tournaments**, and the **state playoffs** that culminate in early December.
