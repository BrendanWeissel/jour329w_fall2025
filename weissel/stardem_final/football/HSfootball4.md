**The Star‑Democrat – Eastern Shore High‑School Football Beat Guide**  
*Prepared for a new reporter covering the schools listed below*  

---

### 1. Why the Eastern Shore beat matters  

The Bayside Conference (the core of the Star‑Democrat’s coverage area) is a micro‑cosm of Maryland high‑school football.  In the past two seasons we have seen:

* **Back‑to‑back state champions** – Stephen Decatur (2023 & 2024).  
* **First‑time unbeaten stretches** – Cambridge‑South Dorchester (first 4‑0 run since 2000; 9‑0 regular season in 2024).  
* **Dramatic turnarounds** – Easton (from 1‑8 in 2023 to an 8‑4 season and two playoff wins in 2024) and Kent Island (from 0‑9 in 2023 to a 5‑0 start in 2024).  

These storylines keep readers coming back, and they also give you a framework for what to watch each week: **program identity, coaching continuity, culture, rivalries, and the historical context that shapes expectations**.  

---

### 2. Season‑long workflow (bullet checklist)

- **Pre‑season** – Compile the “coach‑quotes” from last year’s press releases (e.g., Rob Burnham on his new title at Cambridge‑South Dorchester, Zach Davis on the “new era” at Kent Island). Store them in a shared spreadsheet for quick reference.  
- **Weekly game‑day** – Arrive at the stadium at least 90 minutes early. Grab the official game‑book from the school’s athletic director (or the district office) and verify the listed head coach, starting line‑up, and any noted injuries.  
- **Post‑game** – Draft a 300‑word “quick‑recap” for the next morning’s e‑mail bulletin (focus on the score, key players, and any coaching sound‑bites). Follow up with a longer feature if a program hits a milestone (e.g., a coach’s 50th win, a school’s first home‑playoff victory).  
- **Monthly** – Produce a “state of the conference” column that threads together the five‑year trends you’ve documented (e.g., Kent County’s 1989‑90 state titles, Queen Anne’s 25‑year run under Donny Graef).  
- **Special projects** – When a program reaches a historic moment (e.g., Easton’s first home playoff win since 1993), schedule a “legacy” interview with former players or the long‑standing AD, then pair it with a current‑season perspective.

### 4. Program snapshots (narrative, not a list)

#### Colonel Richardson  
Colonel Richardson’s identity this decade has been defined by its **hard‑hitting ground game** and a **defensive mindset**.  Under James Jackson, the 2024 squad rebounded from a 23‑0 loss to Cambridge‑South Dorchester early in the season and then posted a **33‑0 win over Lancaster** and a **51‑6 rout of Snow Hill**.  The program’s most recent high‑water mark was a **55‑0 victory over Washington in 2022**, a game that still resonates in the locker room.  Rivalries that surface each year are **Kent County** (a close 44‑0 loss in 2024) and **Cambridge‑South Dorchester** (the 23‑0 defeat that sparked the 2024 turnaround).  

#### Cambridge‑South Dorchester  
The Vikings have become the **standard‑bearer of consistency** on the Eastern Shore.  Rob Burnham took over in July 2024, inheriting a squad that finished **9‑0 in the regular season** and earned the **No. 1 overall seed** in Class 1A.  Highlights include a **49‑21 win over Century**, a **46‑8 demolition of Kent County**, and a **42‑0 shutout of Washington**.  Historically, the program’s last **four‑game unbeaten stretch** was in 2000; Burnham has now **matched and exceeded that mark**.  The Vikings’ fiercest rivalry is with **Colonel Richardson**, a matchup that decided the Class 1A East Region second‑round playoff (14‑7 loss).  The team’s identity is built on a **run‑first offense (Sterling Perkins, Anthony Hughes) and a defense that has posted three shutouts this season**.  

#### Snow Hill  
Snow Hill entered the 2024 season as a **Bayside Conference opponent** that fell 51‑6 to Colonel Richardson.  While the record does not detail a championship pedigree, the program is a **steady participant** in the conference schedule and serves as a measuring stick for the larger programs.  

#### Washington  
Washington’s 2024 season is best remembered for a **42‑0 loss to Cambridge‑South Dorchester** in the first round of the Class 1A playoffs.  The game underscored the growing gap between the Vikings and many of the conference’s mid‑tier teams.  

#### Kent County  
Kent County carries a **rich heritage**: the **1989‑90 Class 1A state championships** are still celebrated, and former star Harold Somerville, now head coach, played on those teams.  After a **0‑9 start in 2023**, the 2024 squad surged to a **5‑0 stretch**, highlighted by a **34‑18 upset of Snow Hill** and a **21‑6 win over Washington**.  The Trojans’ identity today is **resilient, belief‑driven football**, a theme Coach Somerville emphasizes in pre‑season talks.  Rivalries are **Colonel Richardson** (a 44‑0 loss), **Cambridge‑South Dorchester** (a 46‑8 loss), **Snow Hill**, and **Washington**.  

#### Stephen Decatur  
The Decatur **Seahawks** are the **definitive power in Class 2A**.  After a **26‑year drought** that ended with a 2023 state title, the program repeated in 2024, finishing **13‑0** and winning a dramatic **13‑12 overtime thriller over Huntingtown** for its second straight championship.  Head coach Jake Coleman, a former assistant on the three‑peat Fort Hill teams of the mid‑1990s, stresses **“playing like Division I athletes.”**  The Seahawks’ identity is a **balanced attack** (quarterback Johnny Hobgood, receivers Davin Chandler, Trybe Wise, and Zakhari Baker) and a **stout, five‑shutout defense**.  Key rivalries are **Westlake** (2024 semifinal opponent) and **Huntingtown** (2024 state‑final rematch).  

#### Wicomico  
Wicomico’s most notable 2024 moment was a **21‑14 upset of Kent Island**, a result that surprised many observers and demonstrated the **depth of talent** in the lower tier of the conference.  

#### Queen Anne’s County  
Under **Brian Mooney**, the Lions blend a **long‑standing tradition** with a **new‑era offensive spread**.  Mooney follows Donny Graef, the **winningest coach in school history (176‑95 over 25 years)**, who now serves as offensive‑line coach.  The 2024 season featured a **20‑15 win over James M. Bennett** and a **24‑0 shutout of Wicomico**.  The Lions’ identity is **grit and “no‑quit” attitude**, a mantra Mooney repeats after each hard‑fought victory.  Rivalries include **James M. Bennett** (the recent home‑coming win) and **Kent County**, a program that edged the Lions 21‑20 in October 2024.  

#### Easton  
Matt Griffith’s **Warriors** turned a **1‑8 season in 2023** into an **8‑4 campaign in 2024**, highlighted by **two playoff victories** – the first home playoff win since 1993 (62‑36 over Parkside) and a **28‑21 second‑round triumph over Kent Island**.  The team’s identity rests on **dual‑threat senior quarterback Mikey O’Connor**, who rushed for **250 yards and three TDs** and threw for **146 yards and two TDs** in the Kent Island game, and **junior running back Dustin Blue**, who contributed **125 yards and three TDs** in the Parkside upset.  Easton’s core rivalries are **Kent Island**, **North Caroline**, and **Queen Anne’s County** (a 27‑26 semifinal win in 2014 that is still recalled).  

#### Kent Island  
Zach Davis inherited a program that went **0‑9 in 2023** and, by mid‑season 2024, **raced to a 5‑0 run**, including a **21‑20 win over Easton** and a **34‑18 victory over Snow Hill**.  The Buccaneers emphasize a **“next‑man‑up” culture** – the 2024 roster features **senior quarterback Gavan Henry** and a **run‑heavy backfield** (Jaxson Hess, Nate Green).  Davis’ philosophy is “keep the football old‑school, physical, and get to the fourth quarter.”  Rivalries are **Easton** (the close 28‑21 loss) and **Queen Anne’s County** (a 21‑20 home defeat).  

#### Parkside  
Brendan Riley’s **Rams** have become the **Cinderella of the conference**, pulling **close victories** (e.g., 37‑30 win over Easton, 21‑20 win over Kent Island) but also **falling short** in key matchups (e.g., 27‑20 loss to Easton in 2024).  The program leans on **running back Karontez Bratten** and a **hard‑working defense**.  Their most frequent opponents are **Easton** and **Kent Island**, both of which have produced nail‑biting finishes.  

#### James M. Bennett  
Greg Lasinski’s **Clippers** have struggled to find consistency in 2024, falling **20‑15 to Queen Anne’s County** and **27‑21 to Easton**.  The program’s standout is **wide receiver Zakai Smullen**, described as “the most under‑recruited player in the Bayside Conference.”  Rivalries are **Queen Anne’s County** (the recent home‑coming win) and **Easton** (the overtime loss).  

#### North Caroline  
The Bulldogs entered 2024 with **new head coach Heath Wilkins** after a mid‑season firing of Nic McMorris.  The team started **0‑5**, with a **43‑6 loss to Queen Anne’s** marking the deepest deficit of the year.  The program’s recent success (a **9‑2 record and a second‑round playoff appearance in 2023**) still looms as a benchmark.  The **senior running back Zymear Smith** has already committed to the University of Maryland, underscoring the talent the program hopes to re‑ignite.  Primary rivalry: **Kent Island** (a 43‑6 loss in October 2024).

### 5. What to watch each week  

| Focus | Questions to ask | Sources to tap |
|-------|------------------|----------------|
| **Program identity** | How does the coach describe the team’s “style” this week? Are there any new “next‑man‑up” stories? | Pre‑game press conference quotes, coach’s social‑media posts |
| **Coaching continuity** | Is the head coach returning, new, or recently promoted? How does that affect practice philosophy? | Coaching bios from school websites, prior season articles |
| **School culture** | What off‑field initiatives (community service, academic awards) are highlighted? | School newsletters, AD statements |
| **Rivalry heat** | Is a traditional opponent on the schedule? What’s the historical win‑loss edge? | Past game recaps (e.g., Easton vs Kent Island, Colonel vs Kent County) |
| **Historical context** | Did the team achieve a milestone (first unbeaten run, first home playoff win) this season? | Season summaries, previous-year articles |

### 6. Story angles that resonate with the Star‑Democrat audience  

1. **“From the Bottom to the Top”** – profiles of programs that reversed a 0‑9 season (Easton, Kent Island).  
2. **“Back‑to‑Back Champions”** – a deep dive on Stephen Decatur’s repeat title run, focusing on coach Jake Coleman’s experience with Fort Hill’s three‑peat.  
3. **“The Gridiron Family”** – exploring how former players (e.g., Harold Somerville, former 1989‑90 champion) stay involved as coaches.  
4. **“Rivalry Re‑ignited”** – preview pieces before marquee matchups (Easton vs Kent Island, Colonel vs Kent County).  
5. **“Coach’s Corner”** – quarterly features on long‑tenured assistants (Donny Graef at Queen Anne’s, Rob Burnham’s rise from assistant to head coach).  

---

### 7. Final tip  

Every game is an **opportunity to connect past to present**.  When you write a game recap, sprinkle in a **quick historical nugget** (e.g., “the Vikings’ 46‑8 win over Kent County was their first 5‑0 start since 2000”) and a **coach’s perspective**.  That blend of **statistical precision, human voice, and local legacy** is exactly what the Star‑Democrat readers expect from Eastern Shore high‑school football coverage.  

Good luck on the beat—remember, the story is never just about the final score; it’s about the **people, the traditions, and the community that live for those moments**.
