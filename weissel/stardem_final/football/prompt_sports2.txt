Create a comprehensive beat guide for a reporter covering Eastern Shore of Maryland high school football for The Star-Democrat.

Audience & Tone:
Write in a business-casual tone as if an experienced Star-Democrat sports editor is walking a new reporter through the realities of the beat. Assume the reporter understands football but does not yet understand the Eastern Shore football ecosystem.

Editorial Focus (Very Important):
	•	Prioritize information relevant to The Star-Democrat’s coverage area and audience.
	•	Emphasize program identity, coaching continuity, school culture, rivalries, and historical context.
	•	De-emphasize individual players
	•	When individuals are mentioned, focus on head coaches, long-tenured assistants, athletic directors, and key program builders rather than current rosters.

Structure & Style Guidelines:
	•	This should read as a narrative guide, not a listicle.
	•	Avoid excessive bullet points; use bullets only for:
	•	Practical checklists
	•	Key contacts
	•	Reporting workflows
	•	“Less is more” — include only information that is accurate and defensible.

Accuracy Requirements (Critical):
	•	All facts must be verified against the provided source material.
	•	Do not infer, speculate, or generalize beyond what the source supports.
	•	If information is unclear or not supported by the source, omit it.
	•	After completing the guide, re-read the entire document to confirm factual accuracy and internal consistency.

School-by-School Breakdown:
For each high school in the Star-Democrat coverage area, include a concise section addressing:
	•	Historical Success (program reputation, notable eras, championships if verified)
	•	Important Contacts (head coach, AD, or other program-level contacts when supported by the source)
	•	Rivalries (list “None” if no clear rivalry is documented)

Source Use:
	•	Rely exclusively on full story material from the provided source.
	•	Do not supplement with outside knowledge unless explicitly instructed.

Final Instruction:
Once the beat book is complete, review it again in full to ensure it is accurate, focused on the Star-Democrat’s needs, and weighted toward long-term program understanding rather than short-term player profiles.
