**The Star‑Democrat – Eastern Shore High‑School Football Beat Guide**  
*Prepared for the new reporter covering the Eastern Shore of Maryland high‑school football programs listed below.*  

---  

### How to Use This Guide  
- **Read each school profile as a narrative**; the text tells you what the program is known for, who runs it, and who its natural opponents are.  
- **Bullet points are only for quick‑reference items** (contact lists, reporting workflow).  
- **All facts come directly from recent Star‑Democrat coverage** – nothing is inferred or speculative. If a detail is not mentioned in the source material, it is omitted.

## 1. Colonel Richardson High  

**Program identity & recent success**  
- Historically a solid 1A program.  In 2021 the Colonels reached the Class 1A state quarterfinals, the first such run in recent memory.  
- 2022 they advanced past the first round with a forfeit win over Washington, then fell to Bohemia Manor in the second round (2022).  
- 2024 has been a turnaround year: after a 23‑0 loss to Cambridge‑South Dorchester (Sept. 27), the Colonels posted a 56‑0 homecoming shutout of Lancaster (Oct. 4) and a 44‑0 win at Kent County (Oct. 4), bringing the record to 6‑3.  

**Important contacts**  
- **James Jackson – Head Football Coach** (quoted repeatedly on strategy, player development and rivalry games).  

**Rivalries** (as documented in recent stories)  
- **Washington** – the 2022 forfeit win is still referenced.  
- **Cambridge‑South Dorchester** – the 23‑0 loss and the 44‑0 win are highlighted as a key rivalry.  
- **Bohemia Manor** – two consecutive playoff defeats (2022, 2023) are noted as recent postseason foes.

## 2. Cambridge‑South Dorchester High  

**Program identity & recent success**  
- First‑year head coach **Rob Burnham** (July 2024) took over a program that finished 9‑3 in 2023 and reached the Class 1A state semifinals.  
- The 2024 Vikings have started **10‑0**, the first unbeaten stretch of this depth since the 2000 season (when they also went 10‑0).  
- They have outscored their last five opponents **187‑14**, posting shutouts of Colonel Richardson (23‑0) and Washington (42‑0).  

**Important contacts**  
- **Rob Burnham – Head Football Coach** (new title, former offensive line coach).  

**Rivalries** (as documented)  
- **Colonel Richardson** – a rivalry that produced a 23‑0 loss (Sept. 27) and a 44‑0 win (Oct. 4).  
- **Kent County** – a 46‑8 win (Oct. 4) that extended the Vikings’ unbeaten streak.  
- **Easton** – a 27‑24 regular‑season win (Nov. 1) that decided the Class 1A East Region title race.

## 3. Kent County High  

**Program identity & recent success**  
- Head coach **Harold Somerville** (entering his third season, 2024).  
- After a 2‑9 campaign in 2022, Kent County posted a **5‑0** start in 2024, highlighted by a 34‑18 upset of Snow Hill in the 2022 Class 1A East Region playoffs.  
- The team runs a “run‑first” philosophy; the 2024 offense relies heavily on its offensive line and the dual‑threat quarterback **Dwan Harris**.  

**Important contacts**  
- **Harold Somerville – Head Football Coach** (quoted on confidence and offensive philosophy).  

**Rivalries** (as documented)  
- **Cambridge‑South Dorchester** – the 46‑8 loss (Oct. 4) is a recent high‑profile matchup.  
- **Colonel Richardson** – a 44‑0 loss (Oct. 4) is noted as a recent rivalry game.  
- **Snow Hill** – the 2022 playoff upset is repeatedly referenced.

## 5. Stephen Decatur High  

**Program identity & recent success**  
- Head coach **Jake Coleman** (son of former Decatur QB Brycen Coleman).  
- **Back‑to‑back Class 2A state champions** (2023 and 2024) – the first Bayside Conference team to win consecutive titles in 26 years.  
- 2024 season: **12‑0** regular season, a 27‑20 overtime win over Westlake in the semifinals, and a 13‑12 overtime victory over Huntingtown for the state title.  

**Important contacts**  
- **Jake Coleman – Head Football Coach** (quoted on poise, preparation and the state‑title chase).  

**Rivalries** (as documented)  
- **Westlake** – the 2024 semifinal (27‑20) is a recent high‑stakes matchup.  
- **Huntingtown** – the 2023 (21‑13) and 2024 (13‑12) state‑title games are noted as a developing rivalry.

## 6. Easton High  

**Program identity & recent success**  
- Head coach **Matt Griffith** (10th season).  
- 2023: a 1‑8 season after losing senior QB Mikey O’Connor early.  
- 2024: rebounded to **8‑4** (including a 6‑0 regular‑season start) and achieved the **first home playoff win since 1993** (62‑36 over Parkside, first‑round).  
- The Warriors have become a regular playoff participant, finishing 7‑3 in 2024 and earning the No. 2 seed in the Class 2A East Region.  

**Important contacts**  
- **Matt Griffith – Head Football Coach** (quoted on program development, turnover issues and playoff outlook).  

**Rivalries** (as documented)  
- **Kent Island** – a 28‑21 homecoming win (Oct. 27) is highlighted as a marquee rivalry.  
- **Queen Anne’s County** – a 27‑26 upset (Oct. 18) that revived a 2014 playoff series.  
- **North Caroline** – a 28‑21 win (Sept. 20) that featured a dramatic fourth‑quarter comeback.

## Practical Checklist for the Beat  

- **Weekly game‑day log** – note date, opponent, venue, final score, key stats (e.g., turnover margin, standout player).  
- **Contact list** – keep an updated spreadsheet of head coaches (and ADs when identified) for each of the 14 schools.  
- **Rivalry calendar** – flag traditional rivalry weeks (e.g., Easton vs Kent Island, Queen Anne’s County vs Kent County) for extra background in previews.  
- **Injury watch** – many stories highlight injuries (e.g., Queen Anne’s County’s multiple line‑ups, Kent Island’s quarterback changes). Update the “injury status” column after each practice report.  
- **Historical reference file** – store the championship years and notable eras (e.g., Decatur 2023‑24 back‑to‑back titles; Cambridge‑South Dorchester unbeaten stretch 2000 & 2024). Use these when writing “look‑back” angles.
